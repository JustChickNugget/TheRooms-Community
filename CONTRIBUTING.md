# Feedback, suggestions and bug reporting.

By submitting feedback, suggestions, or bug reports, you agree that:

1. You authorize the organization to use your ideas and suggestions to change or improve the game.
2. You do not claim copyright, compensation, or royalties if your suggestions are implemented in the game.
3. You grant the organization permission to publicly use your GitHub username in changelogs as a thank you for your contribution.

If you do not want the organization to credit you for your contribution, please let us know:

- **In Issues:** uncheck the "Allow mention in changelogs" checkbox.
- **In Discussions:** simply mention in your post whether you want to be credited or not.

If you do not specify your preference, your username may be mentioned by default.
